# Man Man Learn Chinese Online — design standard

- Coffee milk board #765D50; cream card #FFFDF7; dark brown text #574238.
- Revealed learning content: English (pink), Chinese (yellow), bold tone-marked Pinyin (peach), in that order.
- Practice card: lesson/topic, speaking prompt, learning content only; all interactive controls outside.
- Two-column controls: Normal green left / Next purple right; Slow blue left / Review Later gray right; Restart dusty red below.
- Normal and slow audio must read the displayed Mandarin text, without appended examples. Quiz answers hidden until revealed or graded.
- Four brief numbered instructions, Home link, encouragement and creator footer. Mobile-friendly and no student profiles or server-side learning history.
- Use theme.css in every activity. New material should reuse lessons.js rather than duplicate vocabulary.
